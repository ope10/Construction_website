# Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 30, 2026

At StructuraNext Engineering Corp. (referred to as "StructuraNext," "we," "us," or "our"), safeguarding the confidentiality and security of your architectural designs, blueprints, and personal credentials is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, and protect your information when you interact with our bidding portals and estimation forms.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you submit a project estimate inquiry or upload tender bids:
- **Contact Credentials**: Representative name, telephone network details, email address, and corporate entity name.
- **Structural Blueprint Payloads**: Architectural drawings, DWG/DXF files, ZIP archives, and PDF specifications up to 50MB.
- **Estimate Requirements**: Division selection, budget allocation pools, and expected timelines.

## 2. Dynamic Data Retention & File Encryption

All blueprint and design files uploaded via our subcontractor bidding portal undergo strict transmission security protocols:
- **Transport Security**: Submissions are transmitted using TLS 1.3 encryption tunnels.
- **Storage Isolation**: Files are immediately routed to isolated cloud buckets with AES-256 at-rest encryption.
- **Retention Thresholds**: Draft files, unaccepted bids, and CAD blueprints are automatically purged from active database servers 90 days after bidding windows close, unless contractually retained.

## 3. Analytical Tracking and Cookies

To optimize load speeds and monitor site performance under WCAG standards, we use essential analytical tracking:
- **Analytical Indicators**: We collect anonymous indicators like page load latencies, image shifts, and filter toggles.
- **Cookie Usage**: Minimal persistent cookies are set to store user form states (e.g., active steps in the estimation form) to prevent data loss. We do not engage in cross-site behavioral tracking or sell telemetry data.

## 4. Subcontractor and Client Disclosures

Intake information is strictly shared with internal estimating teams, project managers, and authorized partner unions. We do not disclose CAD files or bidding parameters to external subcontractors, ensuring compliance with trade secrets and project integrity.
